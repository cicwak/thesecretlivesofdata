**Deeper/model:** retained exactly-once warning, retention risk, normal persistence/replication requirements and blocking behavior. The deterministic model intentionally omits cursor scanning and retention deletion and labels its abbreviated replies. Native tests confirm claim A→B, XACK leaving XLEN unchanged, NOACK omitting the PEL, and trimming followed by XAUTOCLAIM returning deleted IDs.
