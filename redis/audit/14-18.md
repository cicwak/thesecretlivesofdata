# Redis 8.0.0 audit: chapters 14–18

**Статус: исправления интегрированы; исходные verdict в таблицах сохранены как история аудита. Итог и ограничения: [README](README.md).**


Audit date: 2026-10-18. Baseline: site commit `cdc375c`; source checkout Redis 8.0.0, commit **e91a340e241cf0abe3c6a0c254214fbe4aa1d95f**. Reviewed both languages' titles, summaries, 30 captions, 30 detail pairs, commands, every deeper paragraph, desktop and compact scenes, choreography, and laboratory reducers/views. Source was read locally at the pinned commit; links below pin the same files and line numbers. Current redis.io documentation is cross-checked against that source, because `/latest/` also documents features newer than 8.0.

This is a reproducible technical review, not a mathematical proof or a promise of universal behavior under every OS, hardware failure, configuration, or third-party module. Animated times are illustrative. The labs deliberately omit networking, real disks, election timing, ACLs, and many failure states.

Verdicts below describe the baseline and the corrections supplied in `fix-14-18.patch`. Step numbers are one-based. `PASS` means the reviewed claim is supported within its stated assumptions; `FIX` records a concrete discrepancy. A passing caption does not automatically make its accompanying scene correct.
