# Postgres

The player, motion vocabulary and presentation follow this repository's Redis
visualization and Ben Johnson's The Secret Lives of Data / Raft, under the MIT
license included alongside this file.

The Postgres explanations, worked examples and diagrams are independently
authored. Technical sources are the PostgreSQL 18 official manual, the pinned
PostgreSQL implementation and Egor Rogov's PostgreSQL 18 Internals (Postgres
Professional / DMK Press, 2026). Source text and book illustrations are not
redistributed. Each lesson links to the relevant source material.
